FAQ

Questions homeowners ask first.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost on the North Shore?

It depends on the size of the kitchen, whether the layout changes, cabinets, countertops and any plumbing or electrical work. That's why we start with a free visit: you get a written estimate for your kitchen, not a generic price range.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The in-home estimate and the written estimate are free, with no obligation to hire us.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. Our own in-house crew does the work, from demo to final walkthrough. No subcontractors, no shortcuts.

Can you change the layout or remove a wall?

Yes. Layout redesign is one of our kitchen services: we remove walls, relocate islands and add peninsulas. Removing or altering a wall needs a building permit, and we coordinate it as part of the project.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope. You get a timeline together with your written estimate, before any work starts.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We coordinate permits and inspections as part of the project.
